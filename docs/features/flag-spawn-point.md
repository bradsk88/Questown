Allow the player to set the town flag as their spawn point.

Right-click the flag with an empty hand (or maybe a bed?) to bind respawn.
This would reinforce the flag as the central hub and encourage players to
build near town. Could also tie into the onboarding — a quest to "set your
home" at the flag.

Considerations:
- Should it override bed spawn or be a separate mechanic?
- Maybe only works if the flag has a welcome mat nearby?
- Could require a room upgrade (e.g. a "home" room) before it works
