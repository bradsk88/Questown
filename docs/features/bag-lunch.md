Cooks unlock the ability to create bagged lunches at some point.

A bagged lunch is simply two food items held together in a single-slot item. Players can take bagged lunches. When they eat the bagged lunch, they eat the first item from it - which updates the NBT list of hwat the lunch "holds" - they get whatever effects come with that food. Once they eat the bagged lunch a second time (thus eating the second food item from its NBT) the bagged lunch is consumed and nothing is left over.

Long leaver jobs, which up until now have required the leaver to make two trips bring two food to the flag, would now require them to bring a bagged lunch to the flag.  

Cooks would have a station for preparing bagged lunches. And, of course, they would need "bags" of some kind.
