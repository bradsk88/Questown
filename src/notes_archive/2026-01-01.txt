# Long Game Ideas:
> What does the player "get" from QT that they can use in MC

### Arena ###
- Player inserts some number of Blocks of Progress to unlock different levels of "battles"
- Maybe it teleports you to a new dimension (for cheat avoidance)
- Spawn some pre-configured set of enemies depending on the level
- Win a treasure with some amount of loot depending on the level

Why: Allows players to get consistent access to dungeon loot.

### Blacksmith, Armorer, Food Producers ###
- Standard Jobs

Why: Creates a "supply chain" to keep player adventuring

### Enchanter ###
- Standard Job

Why: Creates a non-XP method for enchanting weapons

### Mapped Gatherer ###
- Standard Job

Why: Creates a method for finding resources that are not nearby

### Mysterious Stranger ###
- Arrives every N days
- Has three random items for trade (from "dungeon" loot table)
- Give them a BOP to get the item(s)

Why: Allows players to get consistent access to dungeon loot.

### Trader ###
- Spawns in Tavern
- Has three items up for trade, all craftable
  - Special files define the min/max range for item quantity
    - By default: range is [1]

Why: Allows players to get access to resources
     (Beyond their own village's abilities)
