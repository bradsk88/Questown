BOP Progress Indicator in Flag UI
==================================

The BOP tab in the flag menu should show a progress indicator toward
the next Block of Progress. For example:

  "Next Block of Progress: 60% (from Hunter working)"

Even a simple progress bar per active villager would help. Currently
the BOP economy is opaque — the player has no visibility into how
BOPs are earned, how many they have, or when the next one will arrive.

A chat notification for BOP deposits has been implemented (see
TownFlagBOPItemHandler.insertItem). This TODO is for the UI-side
progress indicator, which is higher effort.

Related v4 proposals:
  - BOP flag particles and tab preselection (v4 Section 4C)
  - Tab indicators / red dots (v4 Section 4E)
